# Interview Preparation

### Why did you choose this project?

I wanted to build a realistic catalog-strategy analytics project rather than a simple charting assignment. The project combines business requirements, data quality, SQL analysis, Python ETL, Excel reporting and Power BI.

### Why SQL?

SQL is useful for profiling relational data and answering repeatable business questions close to the database layer.

### Why Python?

I used Python/Pandas for cleaning, type conversion, duplicate handling, derived fields and reproducible ETL.

### Why Power BI?

Power BI provides interactive filtering and a management-friendly reporting layer.

### What is the biggest limitation?

The dataset is catalog metadata and has no revenue, viewing, subscription or cost information. I therefore avoid claiming financial or customer outcomes.

### How did you validate your dashboard?

I calculated key metrics independently in SQL and compared them with Power BI measures before presenting the dashboard.

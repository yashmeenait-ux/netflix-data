# Business Analysis Report

## Executive Summary

The cleaned Netflix catalog contains 6,232 unique usable titles: 4,263 Movies and 1,969 TV Shows. The catalog represents 113 country labels and 42 category labels. Average movie duration is 99.12 minutes among movies with valid duration data.

## Observed Findings

1. Movies are the larger format in the supplied catalog.
2. The catalog contains substantial geographic and category breadth.
3. Monthly additions can be used to understand when catalog expansion was most concentrated.
4. Category and country relationship tables are multi-valued, so a title can contribute to multiple categories or countries.

## Business Interpretation

These findings describe catalog composition, not commercial success. A high number of titles from a country or category does not prove high demand, profitability or viewing engagement.

## Recommended Follow-up

If business data becomes available, join catalog metadata with:

- Viewing hours
- Subscription engagement
- Revenue
- Content acquisition cost
- Regional performance
- Customer retention

This would allow analysis of content ROI and customer impact.
